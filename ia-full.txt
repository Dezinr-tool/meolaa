Objective

Key functionalities / sections
Hero → Positioning statement → How we build brands → Brand ecosystem → Traction band → Platform teaser → Press → Investors & partners → Newsletter → Where to next → Footer
1.1 Editorial hero
Full-bleed background video, muted autoplay, with poster-frame fallback and separate mobile crop. Eyebrow label (MEOLAA), primary headline, scroll-down cue.
Secondary layer beneath the fold: short positioning paragraph, two CTAs — one routing to Our Story, one opening a brand film in a modal player.
1.2 Vision / positioning statement
Standalone typographic section. Section label, single large statement of the operating-system thesis, supporting sentence.
1.3 'How we build brands' pillars
Numbered pillar set (01, 02, 03…), each with an image, a headline and one supporting line. Desktop presents as an expanding/hovering set; mobile presents as individual cards each carrying its own "Know More" link. Two component variants.

Horizontal scroller introducing the portfolio. Section label, brand name strip (HIRA · Brand 02 · Brand 03), continuous image band.
Below the scroller, brand cards:
Live brand card — logo, name, category, one-line descriptor, image, CTA to Brand Detail or external brand site
Upcoming brand card — same shell, "Coming Soon" state, CTA disabled
Card set is CMS-managed so additional brands can be added without a rebuild.
1.5 Traction / metrics band
Three to four figures, each with a large numeral and a two-to-three line qualifier beneath. Sits against a contrasting background.
1.6 Platform teaser
Section label (MEOLAA LAB), headline, four numbered capability panels navigated by tab or scroll (01–04), each with a full-bleed image, sub-headline and one supporting line. CTA into The Platform.
1.7 Press logos + featured news
Publication logo row, followed by two to three featured release cards pulled live from the Press & Media collection — each with category tag, date, headline and Read More. CTA to view all.
1.8 Investor / partner strip
Section label, headline, supporting line. Institutional investor logo grid. CTA block routing to Partners.
1.9 Newsletter
Email capture field, validation, success and error states, consent line. Requires a named ESP.

6. Partners / Investors
Objective
Communicates Meolaa's investor backing and partnership opportunities across supply, distribution and brand collaboration. Builds trust with prospective investors and strategic partners with clear contact routing.
Key functionalities / sections
Hero → Investors → Partnership value proposition → Partner types → How to engage → Testimonials → Enquiry form → Investor updates → Where to next → Footer
6.1 Hero
Eyebrow with founding marker, section label (PARTNERS), headline, intro paragraph. Stat band of three figures. Two anchor CTAs — one to investors, one to announcements.
6.2 Investor logos
Section label, headline, supporting line.
Two distinct treatments:
Institutional investors — logo grid
Individual / strategic investors — person cards with photo, name and descriptor line
6.3 Partnership value proposition
Headline and supporting statement framing why partners work with Meolaa.
6.4 Partner types
Partnership routes presented as a set, each with its own label, headline and contact action. Indicative types: supply and manufacturing, distribution and retail, brand collaboration.
6.5 How to engage
Numbered process steps from first contact through to agreement.
6.6 Testimonials
Quote blocks with attribution — name, title, organisation, logo or portrait.
6.7 Enquiry / contact form
Fields: name, organisation, email, partnership type (select), message. Validation, error and success states, spam protection, routing by partnership type to the correct internal recipient, confirmation state.
6.8 Investor updates
Filtered feed pulled from the Press & Media collection showing funding and investor-relevant categories only. Each entry with date, category, headline. CTA to view all releases.
7. Careers
Objective
Presents Meolaa's culture, ways of working and open roles to attract high-calibre talent. Prioritises an authentic, energetic portrayal of the team and a frictionless path to apply.
Key functionalities / sections
Culture hero → How we work → Open roles → Values → Benefits → Life at Meolaa → Leadership → The team → Where to next → Footer
7.1 Culture hero
Full-bleed image or video. Eyebrow (CAREERS), headline, intro paragraph, two anchor CTAs — one to open roles, one to values. Scroll cue.
7.2 How we work
Section label, headline, framing paragraph. Numbered set (01, 02, 03) covering the functional areas talent can join, each with a heading and one supporting line.
7.3 Open roles list
Section label, live role count in the heading, framing line.
Filters: function and location. Filter state reflected in URL.
Each role row carries: title, function, location, employment type, CTA to Role Detail.
Speculative application CTA beneath the list for candidates who don't see a matching role.
Requires a decision on ATS integration — hosted role pages fed by an ATS, hosted role pages managed in CMS, or outbound links.
7.4 Values
Numbered value set (01–05), each a headline with optional supporting line.
7.5 Benefits
Benefit set with icon or numeral, heading and one supporting line.
7.6 Life at Meolaa gallery
Image gallery or horizontal scroller. Captions optional. Video entries supported.
7.7 Leadership
Two tiers, both using the shared person card:
Founders — displayed at full size, photo, name, title, LinkedIn
Wider leadership — carousel with position counter
7.8 The team
Team leads grouped by function, each group carrying a function name and a lead count. Person cards within each group, carousel-navigated where the group exceeds the visible set.
