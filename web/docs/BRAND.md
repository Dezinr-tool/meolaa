## Typography

- **Headlines:** Poppins — Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold
- **Body / sublines / CTAs:** Acumin Variable Concept (SemiCondensed, Condensed sparingly, Regular). Web fallback until Adobe kit: **Source Sans 3**
- Acumin kerning ~0–10

## Logo

- Wordmark MEOLAA with custom E (arrow bars)
- Signature colours: Planet Blue + Ecru; prefer Planet Blue logo
- Min size: 8px digital / 3mm print
- Don’ts: no stretch, tilt, shadow, gradient, isometric, stacked letters, non-brand colours
