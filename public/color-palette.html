Meolaa Color Palette

All official brand colors and semantic tokens currently wired on the website. Screenshot this page for client planning — the “Suggested usage” column in docs/COLOR-PALETTE.md is left blank for workshop notes.

Primary

Core brand trio — use in greatest proportion per brand guidelines (Planet Blue, Joyous Yellow, Ecru, White).

Planet Blue

#002F3A

--color-planet-blue · --color-primary · --color-ink · --color-bg

Current usageDefault dark shell; Hero overlay; Loop section background; Inner-page hero bands; Inverse CTAs (Planet Blue on Ecru); Nav dark glass; Logo fill on light nav; Video modal; About roadmap milestones; Founding section ink; Portfolio ink

Joyous Yellow

#FDF28C

--color-joyous-yellow · --color-secondary

Current usagePrimary CTA buttons (yellow fill, blue text); Loop path track/stroke; Founding section CTA; About roadmap path; Button focus ring; Portfolio decorative shape; Press/Founders accent highlights

Ecru

#F8ECE4

--color-ecru · --color-cream · --color-surface · --color-vision-bg · --color-lab-bg · --color-fg

Current usageVision section ground; Warm section backgrounds; Default text on dark (--color-fg); Footer copy & links; Ghost button text; Hero body on dark; Nav dark-glass border tint; Logo ecru fill on dark nav

White

#FFFFFF

--color-white

Current usageInner pages page background; Press section; Footer pre-reveal stage; Nav light glass (50% white); Card surfaces; Section titles on dark (--section-head-on-dark-ink); Green CTA text
